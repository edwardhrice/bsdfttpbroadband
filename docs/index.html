Gigabit broadband in Barton St David

A page by Edward Rice supporting Barton St David Parish Council's campaign to bring full-fibre (FTTP) broadband to the village. It shows, for every premises in the parish, what the government's own data says about gigabit availability and plans.

    Note

    The government body that publishes this data, Building Digital UK (BDUK), describes the plans as provisional. Premises marked "not yet confirmed" are under review and may be removed from the Project Gigabit scope. Dates can slip. BDUK does not guarantee the accuracy of the data, and it can be wrong for individual premises. If your premises is shown incorrectly, check it with the official checker below.
