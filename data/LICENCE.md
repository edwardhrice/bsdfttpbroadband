The files in `data/` (and the copies in `docs/data/`) are derived from:

- BDUK, *OMR and premises in BDUK plans* (UPRN-level release), and
- Ordnance Survey, *OS Open UPRN* (coordinates in `uprn_coords.csv`).

Both are published under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/), and these derived files are released under the same licence.
